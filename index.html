Cairn: Daily Bible Reading

A simple iPhone app for reading the Bible a few minutes a day. Follow a reading plan, mark each day as read and keep your streak going.

  • Short daily readings, about 3–5 minutes
  • World English Bible and King James Version
  • Daily streaks, achievements and gentle reminders
  • Large, adjustable text
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.
