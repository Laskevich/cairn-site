Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Cairn: Daily Bible Reading ("the app") is developed by Laziz Safaev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your reading history stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your reading progress, streak, achievements, bookmarks, notes and settings (such as text size and reminder time) locally on your iPhone. This information is not sent to us or to any server we operate. We do not have accounts, sign-in or a backend server.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

The app does not contain analytics tools, advertising, or tracking technologies, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules them locally on your device. No data is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your payment details. To check subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives information about your purchases, such as an anonymous app user identifier, your subscription status and purchase history, and basic technical information about your device (for example, device model and iOS version). It does not receive your Bible reading history, notes or bookmarks. RevenueCat's handling of this information is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Bible text

The Bible translations in the app (World English Bible and King James Version) are in the public domain and are included in the app itself. Reading them does not require an internet connection and is not reported to anyone.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because your reading data is stored only on your device, you control it: deleting the app removes it. If you have questions about the limited purchase information processed by RevenueCat, or want to exercise privacy rights you may have under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us using the email address below and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
