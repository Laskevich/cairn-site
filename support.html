Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Cairn and tap Cancel Subscription. If you cancel during the free trial, you will not be charged.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the app, go to Profile and tap Restore Purchases.

How is my streak counted?

Your streak grows by one for each calendar day (in your local time) on which you mark at least one reading as read. You get one free streak freeze each week.

Where is my reading progress stored?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so deleting the app also deletes your progress, bookmarks and notes.

Which Bible translations are included?

The World English Bible (WEB) and the King James Version (KJV). Both are in the public domain. The WEB text comes from eBible.org.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Cairn in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that a reminder time is set in the app's Profile tab.
